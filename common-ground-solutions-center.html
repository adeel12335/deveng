Common Ground Solutions Center

Transformative, Systemic, and Compassionate Solutions for Development and Peace

A mission-driven consultancy and capacity-building platform supporting community development, resilience, climate security and peacebuilding.

Mission

CGSC partners with communities, governments and allied organizations to co-create integrated, participatory and systems-aware solutions to complex development and peacebuilding challenges.

Vision

The Center envisions communities that are resilient, resourceful and just, with the capacity to solve challenges, sustain livelihoods, adapt to ecological and political risks, cultivate peace and live in harmony with people and planet.

Our 4Rs Solutions

The right solutions

Designed with clarity of purpose and minimum adverse impact on people and ecosystems.

Rightly done

Co-created with communities while respecting culture, knowledge and lived experience.

For the right reasons

Grounded in ethical intent and long-term thinking rather than short-term expediency.

The right mindset

System-aware, reflective and respectful of context and scale.

How We Work: A Systems-Based Approach

The methodology connects peace, sustainability and climate security. System dynamics, transdisciplinary collaboration and adaptive management are used to help stakeholders explore complex problems, feedback loops and leverage points.

Our Services

Strategic Design & Planning

Community diagnostics, participatory assessment, feasibility studies, scenario planning and multi-stakeholder engagement.

Project Implementation

Project design and management, monitoring and evaluation, adaptive learning and systems re-alignment.

Modeling & Analytics

System dynamics modeling, nexus mapping, leverage-point identification and resilience forecasting.

Capacity Building & Education

Training, professional education and development of practical toolkits and knowledge platforms.

Who We Work With

Partners include non-profit organizations, governments and public institutions, foundations and donors, communities and grassroots networks.
